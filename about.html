Our neighborhood story

North Star Bakery is a place to pause, pick up something handmade, and connect over food. We welcome commuters looking for breakfast, families choosing dinner bread, and neighbors planning celebrations.

Thoughtful ingredients

Our sample sourcing philosophy favors seasonal ingredients and nearby suppliers when available. Ask about the ingredients in a particular item before ordering, especially when managing an allergy.
